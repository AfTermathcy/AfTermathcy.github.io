abstract: "We provide a new mixed integer linear programming formulation for the problem of maximizing the expected number of transplants in kidney exchange with cancellations. Our formulation uses O(|E| + |G ⟲ |) variables and O(|V| + |E| + |G ⟲ |) constraints, where |E| is the number of edges in the compatibility graph, |V| is the number of vertices, and |G ⟲ | is the number of cycles of allowed size. This improves upon the best known formulation, which uses O(L|E| + |G ⟲ |) variables and O(L|V| + L|E| + |G ⟲ |) constraints, where L is a predetermined chain length cap. Another advantage of our formulation is that it does not require capping chain lengths in advance. Our formulation is based on a modification of the mixed integer linear program provided by Dickerson et al. [2020], where we drop the position-indexing. This is a partial draft: additional results and computational experiments will be added soon."
